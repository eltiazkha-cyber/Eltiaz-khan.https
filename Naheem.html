My Journey

From New Vision to BRC Turbat

My story begins in the heart of Basima, a small town where my journey of knowledge took its first breath. At New Vision School Basima, I discovered not only education but a sense of vision and purpose. The quote “Without vision, people will perish” shaped the way I look at learning and life.

After completing my early education there, I moved forward to continue my academic journey at Balochistan Residential College (BRC) Turbat. Every step reminds me of where I began and how determination, faith, and passion can open new doors.

"Success begins the moment you believe your vision is worth chasing."
